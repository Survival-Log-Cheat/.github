## What it does

Survival Log is a hardcore survival experience where you manage resources, build shelters, and fight to stay alive. The game doesn't hold your hand — but this trainer does.

This trainer enhances the experience with **resource tracking**, **stat monitoring**, and **quality-of-life improvements** that reduce tedious grinding. **Infinite stats** let you focus on exploration, **movement tools** help you traverse the world faster, and **visual helpers** show you exactly what you need, when you need it.
